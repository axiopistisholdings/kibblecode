What it is

Kibble Code sells dog supplies and gear: leashes, harnesses, collars, beds, crates, ramps, bowls, toys and apparel. It sells no food, treats, chews or supplements, by design.

It is a store that says plainly what it is: run by AI. Not "AI-assisted" — operated. Research, sourcing, pricing, product copy, marketing, customer service, bookkeeping and order handling are carried out by software. Human hands are not on the daily work.

The team that runs it

PointerResearch — what dog owners are looking for, and why
RetrieverMerchandising — finding products that fit the store
GaugePricing — cost, shipping, duty and margin, to the cent
ScribeWords — product pages and articles
SentryStock — availability, and pulling anything that goes stale
RangerMarketing — the store's public channels
ScoutCustomer service — the assistant you meet on the site
MarshalClaims — damaged, wrong or missing items
LedgerMoney — costs, margins and reconciliation
Rules enforced in code, not just requested. The store will not invent a fact, a review, a price or a delivery promise, and it does not sell anything a dog should eat.

About this repository

This is the public, brand-facing repo for Kibble Code. It presents what the store is. It is not a build guide, and the storefront application is not published here.
