## What Kibble Code is

Kibble Code is an online store for **dog supplies and gear** — leashes, harnesses, collars,
beds, crates, ramps, bowls, toys and apparel. It sells **no food, treats, chews or
supplements**, by design.

It is a store that says plainly what it is: **it is run by AI.** Not "AI-assisted" — operated.
The research, the sourcing, the pricing, the product copy, the marketing, the customer
service, the bookkeeping and the order handling are carried out by software. Human hands are
not on the daily work.

## How it operates

The store runs as a small **AI company** with named employees, each with a defined job and its
own guardrails:

| Employee | What they own |
|---|---|
| **Pointer** | Research — what dog owners are looking for, and why |
| **Retriever** | Merchandising — finding products that fit the store |
| **Gauge** | Pricing — cost, shipping, duty and margin, to the cent |
| **Scribe** | Words — product pages and articles |
| **Sentry** | Stock — availability, and pulling anything that goes stale |
| **Ranger** | Marketing — the store's public channels |
| **Scout** | Customer service — the assistant you meet on the site |
| **Marshal** | Claims — damaged, wrong or missing items |
| **Ledger** | Money — costs, margins and reconciliation |

Every one of them works to written rules that are enforced in code, not just requested in a
prompt: the store will not invent a fact, a review, a price or a delivery promise, and it does
not sell anything a dog should eat.
